# Post-Publish Feedback

Use this after a video is uploaded or when analytics, comments, retention graphs, CTR, or watch-time data are available.

The goal is to update the factory format, not merely explain whether one video did well.

## Required Output

```text
Post-Publish Feedback
- Winning signal:
- Weak timestamp:
- Likely cause:
- Next test:
- Format update:
- What not to overfit:
```

## Inputs To Ask For Or Use

- title and thumbnail
- impressions and CTR
- average view duration
- retention graph or drop timestamps
- first 3-second hold
- 15-30 second hold
- rewatch spikes
- comments and repeated viewer phrases
- subscribers gained
- end screen or link clicks

## Diagnosis Map

### Low CTR

Likely issue:

- title/thumbnail emotion is weak
- promise is too broad
- no concrete number/object/person

Fix:

- sharpen click emotion
- add one concrete anchor to title/thumbnail
- test a narrower viewer

### High CTR, Early Drop

Likely issue:

- opening does not match pre-click state
- hook promised drama but script started with explanation
- anchor appears too late

Fix:

- move anchor into first 5-15 seconds
- open inside the mistake or irreversible moment

### Mid-Video Drop

Likely issue:

- reward loop went flat
- list structure replaced story movement
- no new question after first payoff

Fix:

- add a 20-30 second clue/reversal/action beat
- remove background paragraphs

### End Drop

Likely issue:

- ending did not recover the anchor
- CTA is generic
- payoff was already complete too early

Fix:

- return to the first object/sentence/action
- make CTA channel-specific

### Strong Comments, Weak Retention

Likely issue:

- topic emotion is strong but pacing is weak

Fix:

- keep topic family
- rewrite rhythm and reward loop

## Format Update Rule

Only change the repeatable channel formula when the same signal appears across at least 3 videos, unless the failure is severe and obvious.

Do not overfit one outlier.

Track updates as:

```text
Old pattern:
Observed signal:
New pattern:
Test on next 3 videos:
```
