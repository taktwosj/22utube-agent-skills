# Pre-Click State

Use this for every serious script, especially mindset, finance, scam prevention, history reversal, and sassy/revenge topics.

The viewer does not click as a blank person. They arrive with a defensive thought, fear, anger, shame, curiosity, or validation need. The opening must answer that state within 15 seconds.

## Required Output

Before writing, output:

```text
Pre-Click State
- Viewer arrives thinking:
- Defensive thought:
- Validation need:
- Fear or desire:
- First 15 seconds must answer:
```

## Common States

- **Defensive thought**: "I already know this, but I still cannot do it."
- **Self-defense**: "I am not stupid. There must be a reason I fell for this."
- **Validation need**: "Tell me my anger is not unreasonable."
- **Anxiety**: "Could this happen to me or my parents?"
- **FOMO**: "Am I late?"
- **Skepticism**: "This sounds exaggerated."
- **Shame**: "I do not want to admit I failed."
- **Reversal hunger**: "I want the person I hate to be exposed."

## Opening Rule

The first 15 seconds must do one of these:

- name the defensive thought directly
- put the viewer inside the mistake
- show the irreversible moment
- reveal the number/object/action that changes the meaning
- make the viewer feel seen without comforting them generically

## Bad vs Good

Bad:

```text
Today's topic is how to avoid voice phishing.
```

Good:

```text
You do not fall for scams because you are stupid.
You fall because the first sentence sounds like a real investigation.
```

Bad:

```text
Do not compare yourself to others.
```

Good:

```text
You already know comparison is useless.
So why do you still open the profile?
```
