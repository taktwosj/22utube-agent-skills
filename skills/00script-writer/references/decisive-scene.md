# Decisive Scene

Use this when the hook feels generic, the script reads well but does not land, or the topic needs one scene that carries the whole video.

The goal is not to write beautifully for its own sake. The goal is to compress the contradiction into a scene, then unpack it clearly while keeping one person, object, or anchor in focus.

## Required Output

Before writing, output:

```text
Decisive Scene
- Core contradiction:
- Scene object/action/number:
- Poetic opening:
- Plain unpacking promise:
- Subject thread to follow until the end:
- Physical plausibility check:
```

## Core Rule

```text
Poetic compression -> subject thread -> plain unpacking -> new reward -> anchor recovery
```

Write the opening like a poem, but do not stay vague. The poem must identify the one person, stock, object, action, sentence, or number the script will keep following. After the compressed scene, explain what it means in plain language. Then keep paying off attention every 10-20 seconds without losing that subject thread.

## Contradiction First

A decisive scene needs a contradiction, not just a dramatic fact.

Good contradiction types:

- **Small thing, huge consequence**: 1미터였는데 벌금 300만원.
- **Gain, but no peace**: 500만원 벌고도 잠 못 자는 사람.
- **Simple action, impossible feeling**: "수고하셨습니다" 한마디가 어렵다.
- **Status, but lowered posture**: 왕실의 며느리였지만 문은 열리지 않았다.
- **Help, but punishment**: 차를 바로 세웠는데 경찰이 왔다.
- **Failure, but new proof**: 사기당한 이야기를 이력서에 썼다.

Weak:

```text
반지하에 살던 상준이는 10년 뒤 서울 아파트를 샀습니다.
```

Stronger:

```text
그는 이력서에 성공을 쓰지 않았습니다.
사기당한 이야기를 썼습니다.
```

## Verse And Unpack

Use short poetic lines for the scene. Then unpack in clear prose.

Pattern:

```text
[Verse]
One action.
One object.
One contradiction.

[Unpack]
Here is what happened.
Here is why it matters.
Here is the next question.
```

Example:

```text
500만원을 벌었다.
그런데 잠이 오지 않았다.

왜냐하면 그는,
1000만원에 팔고 나온 비트코인이
1억이 되는 걸 봤기 때문입니다.
```

Do not turn the whole script into poetry. Use verse for hooks, reversals, and endings. Use plain prose for facts, procedures, and evidence.

## Show By Action

Do not explain the emotion first. Show the action that makes the emotion obvious.

Weak:

```text
그녀는 완전히 고립됐습니다.
```

Stronger:

```text
음식이 들어온다.
말해도 반응은 없다.
물이 들어와도, 반응은 없다.
```

Weak:

```text
그는 억울했습니다.
```

Stronger:

```text
1미터였다.
차는 똑바로 섰고,
남자는 집으로 올라갔다.

며칠 뒤, 우편물이 왔다.
300만원.
```

Use emotion words only after the scene has earned them.

## Keep The Hook Subject

If the hook opens with one person, keep following that person.

Bad:

```text
500만원 벌고도 잠 못 자는 사람.
Then explain Bitcoin history from 2009.
```

Good:

```text
500만원 벌고도 잠 못 자는 사람.
Follow what he bought, where he sold, what he saw after selling, and why that memory made him buy late.
```

Rule:

- If the hook starts with **a person**, follow the person.
- If it starts with **an object**, keep returning to the object.
- If it starts with **a number**, make the number change meaning across the script.
- If it starts with **a sentence**, recover the sentence near the ending.

## Subject Thread

The poetic opening is not decoration. It chooses the thread the whole script must hold.

Name the thread before drafting:

```text
Subject thread:
- Samsung Electronics as a price/button/holder story
- Sangjun as the person who wrote failure on a resume
- one closed door in a palace punishment
- 1 meter in a parking lot
- "일정 확인하고 말할게" as a refusal action
```

Every section must answer one question:

```text
What changed for this same subject?
```

Bad:

```text
Hook: 500만원 벌고도 잠 못 자는 사람.
Body: Explain Bitcoin history, blockchain, halving, exchange trends.
```

Good:

```text
Hook: 500만원 벌고도 잠 못 자는 사람.
Body: Follow his buy price, sell price, what he saw after selling, why the missed profit grew bigger than the real profit, and what he should check before buying late again.
```

Bad:

```text
Hook: 삼성전자 매수 버튼 앞 3초.
Body: Explain semiconductor cycles broadly.
```

Good:

```text
Hook: 삼성전자 매수 버튼 앞 3초.
Body: Keep returning to the finger over the button, the price on screen, the news phrase that moved the finger, and the one question before pressing.
```

If the script leaves the thread for broad background, cut the background or turn it into a short reward that returns to the thread immediately.

## Physical Plausibility

The decisive scene must make physical sense. If the viewer asks "how is that possible?" for the wrong reason, immersion breaks.

Bad:

```text
작은 문구멍으로 밥그릇이 들어왔다.
```

Safer:

```text
문은 열리지 않았다.
기록에는 문에 구멍을 내어 음식과 물을 넣었다고 전한다.
중요한 건 구멍의 크기가 아니다.
사람은 들어가지 못했고, 음식만 들어왔다는 사실이다.
```

When the exact size, layout, or movement is unknown, describe the consequence instead of inventing the mechanism.

## Reward Loop

The opening scene is not enough. Keep rewarding the viewer without leaving the subject.

Use this loop:

```text
0:00 contradiction scene
0:10 plain meaning
0:20 next concrete detail
0:40 small reversal
1:00 bigger question
then repeat: scene/detail -> meaning -> new question
```

Every reward must still point back to the same person, object, number, or sentence. Do not escape into broad background.

## Final Test

Before drafting, ask:

```text
Can I summarize the whole video as:
"This is not really about ___. It is about ___."
```

Examples:

- This is not really about 1 meter. It is about when a harmless-looking action legally becomes driving.
- This is not really about Bitcoin price. It is about why missed profit hurts more than real profit.
- This is not really about a palace punishment. It is about how power closes the door before it gives the sentence.

If this sentence is weak, the script will read but not land.
