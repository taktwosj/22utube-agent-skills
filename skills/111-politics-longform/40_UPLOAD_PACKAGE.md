# Upload Package

Do not apply Shorts `#shorts` title rules to political longform.

Format:

```text
제목
{person/source issue hook}｜{viewer reason to click}

내용
출처 {실제 채널명} YYYY.MM.DD

{one-paragraph summary of what this edit explains}

00:00 {section explanation}
02:20 {section explanation}
07:46 {section explanation}
14:16 {section explanation}

출처
- 원본 채널:
- 원본 영상:
- 원본 URL:
- 원본 업로드일:

{3-5 hashtags}
```

Timestamp lines must explain what the section argues and why it matters. Do not
use bare chapter labels.
